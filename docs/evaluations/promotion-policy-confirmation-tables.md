## 8. Limits

* 16 assets from 2 discovery seeds on one model and a simulated benchmark; sets A and B share the P3/P4 tasks and the baseline cache, so they are replicates of the pipeline, not independent samples of tasks.
* Truth comes from one repetition per task of a forced-ACTIVE run; it shares the noisy-baseline property of the regression estimate and is itself a statistical label, not an oracle.
* The replay of the sequential rule is simulated on a full grid; the actual-savings claims here are limited to the measured cache reuse.
* The policy parameters were frozen before these data; they were not changed.
