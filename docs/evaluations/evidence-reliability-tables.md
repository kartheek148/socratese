Generated by `ee-ctl` (`drift-calibrate`, `drift-sim`, `drift-real`, `drift-report`) from the frozen protocol in [evidence-reliability-design.md](../experiments/evidence-reliability-design.md). Narrative, caveats and the decision: [evidence-reliability-report.md](./evidence-reliability-report.md). **All drift in sections 1-4 and 6 is synthetic** (a labelled overlay on the alternative's observed outcomes); section 5 uses real model outcomes with synthetic overlays. Thresholds were calibrated on development seeds 100000-100199 ([evidence-reliability-calibration.md](./evidence-reliability-calibration.md)) and frozen before the held-out seeds 7000000+ were run. ### Drift precision and recall (monitor-only, pooled over S1-S10, S10b excluded)

A stream is a *positive* when its ground truth has at least 50 materially wrong positions; a *true alarm* is a DRIFTED transition after the onset; a *false alarm* is a DRIFTED transition at a position where the evidence is not materially wrong. Streams: 3602; true positives 2066, false negatives 34, streams with a false alarm 9, clean negatives 1493. **Recall 98.4%**, **precision 99.6%** (stream-level; a stream with both a late true alarm and an early false one counts in both), false alarm events per 1,000 decisions 0.004. The ground truth is synthetic: these are properties of the frozen scenarios, not of production drift.
